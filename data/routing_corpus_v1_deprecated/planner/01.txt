<channel|>Migrating from MySQL to PostgreSQL with **zero downtime** is one of the most complex operations in database engineering. Because the two engines use different SQL dialects, data types, and replication protocols, you cannot use standard built-in replication.

To achieve zero downtime, you must use a **Change Data Capture (CDC)** pattern. This allows the new database to "catch up" with the old one while the application is still writing to the original.

Here is the professional architectural breakdown of the migration process.

---
