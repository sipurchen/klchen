<channel|>Estimating software development time is notoriously difficult because "basic" is subjective. However, for a professional-grade, production-ready (not just a prototype) REST API, a realistic estimate for a single senior engineer is **5 to 10 engineer-days.**

Here is the breakdown of the estimation based on a standard lifecycle: **Design $\rightarrow$ Implementation $\rightarrow$ Testing $\rightarrow$ Deployment/Documentation.**

---

### 1. The Breakdown of Tasks

#### Phase 1: Architecture & Schema Design (1 Day)
Before writing code, you must define the "contract."
*   **Data Modeling:** Designing the database schema (SQL tables or NoSQL collections).
*   **API Contract:** Defining endpoints, request/response bodies, and status codes (OpenAPI/Swagger spec).
*   **Tech Stack Setup:** Initializing the repository, setting up linting, pre-commit hooks, and CI/CD pipelines.
