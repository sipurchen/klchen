<channel|>The speed of light in a vacuum is approximately **299,792,458 meters per second**.

In common scientific and everyday use, it is often rounded to:
*   **3.00 × 10⁸ m/s** (Scientific notation)
*   **300,000 km/s** (Kilometers per second)
*   **186,282 miles per second** (Miles per second)<end_of_turn>
