<channel|>To select the top 5 customers by their total order amount, you need to group the records by `customer_id`, sum the `amount` for each group, sort them in descending order, and then limit the results to 5.

Here is the SQL query:

```sql
SELECT 
    customer_id, 
    SUM(amount) AS total_order_amount
FROM 
    orders
GROUP BY 
    customer_id
ORDER BY 
    total_order_amount DESC
LIMIT 5;
```

### Explanation:
1.  **`SELECT customer_id, SUM(amount)`**: This selects the customer identifier and calculates the sum of all their individual order amounts.
2.  **`AS total_order_amount`**: This gives the calculated sum a descriptive alias, making it easier to reference in the `ORDER BY` clause.
3.  **`FROM orders`<end_of_turn>
