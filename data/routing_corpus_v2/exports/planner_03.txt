The riskiest step in any migration is the **Data Cutover**—the moment you transition the "source of truth" from the old provider to the new one. If this fails, you face either permanent data loss or an extended outage that could break your business.

Here is the trade-off comparison between the two primary strategies for a small team.

### Trade-off: Data Cutover Strategies

| Comparison Criteria | **Approach A: "The Big Bang"** (Stop $\rightarrow$ Move $\rightarrow$ Start) | **Approach B: "The Parallel Sync"** (Live Replication $\rightarrow$ Switch) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Engineering Effort (Small Team)** | **Minimal.** Best suited for teams with limited time and a requirement for high data integrity over high availability. | **Significant.** Requires deep expertise in networking and database internals to ensure the sync is healthy before the cutover. |

### Which one should you choose?

*   **Choose "The Big Bang" if:** You are managing a small app where users can tolerate an hour of maintenance downtime (e.g., a 2:00 AM window) and your primary goal is to ensure 100% data integrity with the least amount of engineering headache.
*   **Choose "The Parallel Sync" if:** Your app is "mission-critical" (e.g., a payment gateway or real-time service) where even 15 minutes of downtime results in significant revenue loss or user churn, and you have the engineering bandwidth to manage the complexity.<turn|>
